## Short description

Cut the strings. Win the crowd. Save the theatre.

Watch a shadow puppet perform, then recreate its motion by cutting a fully simulated marionette's strings. Rehearse three theatrical acts, master the timing, and take the stage for a Grand Finale with no guiding shadow. Earn more than 90% to win the crowd and keep the theatre alive. Mr Bellamy has notes. His patience is running out.

## Theme interpretation

Director's Cut is both literal and structural: the player directs a performance by choosing where and when to cut strings. Rehearsal turns a reference performance into learned timing; the premiere tests the player's own final cut.
