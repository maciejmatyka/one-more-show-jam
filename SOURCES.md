## Release-candidate compliance note

The full rules restrict third-party trademarks/logos (section 15(d)), while the provider's FREE & DEMO page asks non-commercial releases to include its intro. RC1 preserves the licensed startup intro. Obtain the organizer/provider's clarification before final contest submission; the partner listing alone is not treated as an explicit exception. This is an identified RC1 handoff item, not a claim of cleared final eligibility. The gameplay video contains no provider logo.

Submission itself must be made by the human entrant under section 17. No automated contest entry has been made.
