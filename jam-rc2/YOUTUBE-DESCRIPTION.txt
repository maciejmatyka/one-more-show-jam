Cut the strings. Win the crowd. Save the theatre.

One puppet. Five strings. One last chance.

Watch the shadow, cut the marionette's strings and recreate its performance using real physics. Master three rehearsals, then step into the Grand Finale with no shadow to guide you. Can you win over the audience and save Mr Bellamy's theatre?
