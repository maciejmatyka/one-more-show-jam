## Reading the figures

- Capture: actual pixels from Unreal, cropped and proportionally resized.
- Fixture: a deliberately selected result used to exercise presentation or progression.
- Schematic: an editorial diagram, never represented as a captured UI.
- Film: automated cuts and actual Chaos scoring; the cursor and soundtrack were editorial layers.
